## Answers to the scientific questions

1. **Does episode recency add information?** It adds an explicit ordering
   representation absent from the reviewed feature inventories. Same-summary
   groups retain different recencies on 1,712/2,653 and 1,531/2,422 observations.
   Whether that information improves prediction beyond existing counts, state
   memory, season and municipal identity is unresolved.
2. **Is there an anticipatory neighbor association?** Not evaluable. A real
   geography admission dependency remains: official 2018 code equality does
   not establish valid historical adjacency, including outside-region neighbors.
   This is no evidence against a spatial association.
3. **Which signals overlap?** Completed-start and completed-end recency describe
   the same run; their difference is completed duration minus one. Only end
   recency is primary. It correlates −0.694/−0.692 with recent excess frequency,
   compared with weaker overlap with existing dengue lags. No new window family,
   duplicate M01/M02 feature, duration search or alternative predictor is selected.
4. **Which patterns persist across years/territories?** Shorter recency accompanies
   onset weakly: pooled Spearman −0.126/−0.097, with 7/8 negative raw directions
   per horizon. Meta 2021 reverses; Tolima's aggregate pattern is stronger.
   Calendar-centered ranks remain negative in all 16 repeated territorial cells,
   pooled −0.098/−0.092. This does not remove local confounding or provide
   independent-year confirmation.
5. **What differs in missed onsets?** G0 misses 167/313 and 157/219 events;
   all-four misses number 125/112. Missed/detected median recencies of 6/5 months
   (T+1) and 5/5.5 (T+2) and overlapping ECDFs do not show a consistent selective
   rescue signal. Shared errors are not an independently sampled subgroup.
6. **What prevents a predictive conclusion?** These are consolidated notifications
   and an experimental H5 proxy, selected non-excess-at-T cohorts and consumed
   years. Repeated episodes/months, shared waves and existing inputs remain
   plausible explanations. Calendar control is descriptive; exact-state-summary
   conditional rank associations are small (−0.038/−0.071) and sparse. There is
   no fitted intervention, prospective test, independently validated outbreak
   identity, availability-delay evaluation or false-alert comparison for O2.
7. **Is a new predictive experiment justified?** There is a narrow question worth
   specifying for O2, rather than demonstrated improvement: does one completed-end
   recency input improve paired detection at a fixed false-alert burden after
   accounting for the existing memory inputs? The EDA supports considering that
   design only. It does not support a broad search, automatic EXP-DELTA02 launch
   or spatial experiment before historical geography admission.

## Support, chronology and feasibility

All 5,075 original evaluation rows have a previously completed observed episode;
all 313/219 onsets remain. The scan finds 1,326 complete runs through 2024-12.
Censored/active runs remain separate from complete runs. The fixed bands retain
at least nine onsets each; annual/departmental cells have at least 17. These
aggregate counts satisfy the declared descriptive support rule, while matched
conditional cells do not. Municipal effects are not estimated: EDA-EXP01's sparse
municipality-year event support remains an important limitation.

Episode clearance and all new signal observations must be strictly before T.
Clearance at T only becomes usable at T+1; an older completed episode may still
supply the last known completed history. Missing states/calendar gaps reset
history; unknown recency is never zero. Prefix invariance and boundary cases
verify the chronology. Future states are used solely to audit the frozen outcome
labels. No post-2024 regional observations enter the calculations.

A scalar O2 recency is technically simple and needs no new source family or
platform. Its maintained state, start/end observation and censoring requirements
still need an explicit feature contract if a later experiment is authorized.
The retrospective snapshots do not show what was delivered at historical T:
notification/adjustment metadata are not complete as-of revision vintages. The
same operational timing limitation affects potential neighbor counts. Geographic
proximity would not establish transmission even after valid geometry admission.
