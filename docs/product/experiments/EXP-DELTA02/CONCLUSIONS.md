## What the new evidence resolves

The [results](RESULTS.md) go beyond EDA-EXP02's attribute-only admission attempt.
Three official national DANE municipal archives were recovered and tested:
2005, 2018 and 2021. All include the exact 76 target codes and valid target and
bounding-box candidate-neighbor polygons. The latter two have no positive-area
target-related overlaps. External neighbors are explicitly included. The
national canonical table contains every potential neighbor in their union.
Thus the primary impediment is not an absent geographic source, missing target
code, department-only proxy, invalid target polygon or lack of national counts.

The measured positive-boundary relation is **not stable across these official
vintages**: eight edges differ between 2005 and 2018, and one more appears in
2021, affecting six target municipalities. Some separations in one vintage become
extended shared boundaries in another. These observations demonstrate map/graph
disagreement, not the dates or legal causes of real territorial change.

No inspected archive, metadata or pinned canonical source supplies a resolved
valid-time crosswalk linking those differing relations to the municipality-month
notification histories. The canonical historical applicability field is explicitly
`UNVERIFIED`. Choosing one graph for every training/evaluation year, or treating
edition years as effective dates of each relation, would introduce an unsupported
scientific assumption. A later publication date alone is not a reason to reject
retrospective use; unresolved historical relationship validity is the reason here.
