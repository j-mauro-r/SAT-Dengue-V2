This record analyzes existing artifacts only. It does not train, score models anew, recalibrate, choose a new threshold, revise H5, or authorize operational use. “Confirmed” denotes checked numerical evidence; “interpretation”, “limitation”, and “hypothesis” distinguish its implications. ## Executive scientific reading

**Confirmed:** current epidemiological information improves pooled T+1 onset detection relative to lagged history in every family (P1: +12.86 to +20.00 pp), with positive direction in each development year. Its incremental value is smaller when historical channel and lagged climate are already present (P2). At T+2, P1 effects span −3.16 to +1.90 pp and do not establish a stable advantage. Historical target-month thresholds show the clearest family-specific contribution: P4 improves boosting detection at both horizons, in every development year, with strictly positive pointwise sensitivity intervals; GAM does not share that pattern. Exact arms, counts, burden and intervals appear in the complete primary inventory below.

**Interpretation:** the experiment supports studying current-state information for near-term detection and historical thresholds for boosting, while keeping detection and realized workload inseparable. Lagged climate is not a general improvement: its T+2 boosting gains accompany substantially more false alerts, and reverse in stress 2024. Removing parts of a full model can be a simplification hypothesis, but a null interval does not establish redundancy or equivalence.

**Limitation:** every primary profile guardrail fails, some department guardrails fail, calibration shifts markedly across years, and none of the primary comparisons receives an approving protocol disposition. Three dependent development years and pointwise intervals cannot establish future utility. The intended consumed-2025 benchmark stopped at its coverage/maturity gate; zero fits there are not proof of completed scoring. No 2025 sensitivity, calibration or transport claim is available.

**Decision:** preserve H5 and the complete evidence inventory; take targeted questions into separately designed EXP04/EXP05 work, without selecting a champion, changing the budget after observing results, or treating 2024/2025 as independent confirmation.

## Scientific question, design and reading keys

Which information contributes to exact-onset detection, conditional on fixed H5 labels, a shared complete cohort, fixed model families, separate train/calibration/selection roles and a common selection-budget policy? Sources are [scope](alcance.md), [design](DESIGN.md), [matrix](ablation-matrix.frozen.yml), [protocol](protocol.json), [earlier analyses](../analisis-1.md), [EXP02 synthesis](../analisis-2.md) and [experiment roadmap](../experimentos.md).

H5 uses strict count > historical same-calendar-month type-7 P75, retaining epidemic years. Eligibility is known Y(T)=0. T+1 onset and excess coincide there; T+2 onset requires Y(T+1)=0 and Y(T+2)=1, while excess only requires Y(T+2)=1. Y(T+1) never determines T+2 admission. The base B contains lagged counts, seasonality and municipality identity; A=U+Z+R0 contains current raw state, current channel and current notification ratio; K=F+L+R− is historical channel context; C− is lagged climate, Cm mixed current/lagged climate. Ratios describe notifications, not standardized incidence or causal risk.

`C(variant,objective,family,horizon,window)` uniquely identifies `B/result.json.children` and its `cell_id`; join that ID to prediction/selection/error tables and terminal artifacts. `P/S/O(id,family,horizon,window)` identifies a row in the corresponding contrast CSV. CSV row numbers below are one-based physical records including the header; no fields contain embedded record breaks. Left/right order is retained everywhere. G/L/X abbreviate gam/lightgbm/xgboost only where explicitly indicated.

Burden is **false onset alerts / monitored municipality-years D**, including covered ineligible months in D. Sensitivity denominator N is common eligible onset events. Precision=TP/(TP+FP); FAR=FP/(TP+FP), undefined when no alerts. Alert-month fraction=(TP+FP)/(12D), not alerts/eligible rows. All deltas are left minus right. Do not sum horizons as independent episodes. Actual issuance lead is unavailable; horizons are nominal.

### Current state versus anticipatory information

P1 answers “does state help?” affirmatively as a T+1 point pattern; P2 shows a smaller conditional addition once K+C− is supplied. ## Defensible interpretations

EXP03 measures **conditional predictive contribution**, not biological causality or uniquely attributable feature importance. Threshold information may help models represent the fixed H5 event boundary; that is not proof of an independent biological predictor or leakage merely because labels also use historical references. Current information appears more useful at nominal T+1 than T+2. Selective simplification is worth testing where a reduced model has a favorable joint point trade-off, but cannot be accepted from non-significance. Full-model performance is not the sum of single-block effects because blocks overlap statistically and models are refitted.

## Limitations

Three dependent development-year clusters, overlapping training histories, finite event support and many pointwise comparisons limit uncertainty. Profile floors are interpretability checks, not power or clinical-effect thresholds. Single-year territory results lack the pooled support requirement. No noninferiority margin, measured operational capacity, real publication vintages, biological causal design or prospective validation exists. Historical profile assignments can change with the training cutoff. Common-case completeness applies only to the admitted population and periods. Recorded code/runtime/input hashes establish lineage but this audit does not revalidate raw-data truth or rerun training. Stored calibration regressions may be unstable on compressed probability ranges. 2025 and common-month/pooled transport performance remain unavailable.
