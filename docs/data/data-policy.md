# Data Policy

## Sources and Current Local Use

Epidemiological and climate snapshots are supplied locally. Notebook 03 produces consolidated individual and monthly outputs; notebook 04 derives separate model-preparation bundles without rewriting those sources. January 2020 climate recovery is incorporated for the reviewed Tolima/Meta scope. Consult the [decision register](../product/traking-concluciones.md) for provenance limits and the [preparation workflow](../product/stage-3/README.md) for implemented identity/preservation controls.

Exact original export lineage, universal upstream retrieval and historical operational snapshots are not established by the local pipelines. DANE reference provenance is recorded with local hashes; this does not certify historical boundary applicability or complete reporting.

## Git Data Boundaries

Do not commit large raw/interim/processed datasets or sensitive personal health records. Small synthetic fixtures, tiny non-sensitive examples and reproducibility metadata/configuration may be versioned. Local artifacts and private backups remain restricted and ignored.

## Retrieval and Deferred Infrastructure

Kaggle/local/Colab remain possible large-data locations, not a universally implemented download chain. Earth Engine recovery evidence does not establish retrieval for every source/version. Reuse current supplied snapshots and recorded identities unless a bounded task authorizes retrieval or data changes.

DVC, S3 and additional orchestration may be introduced only for demonstrated traceability, collaboration or deployment needs. Existing local manifests, preservation checks and immutable bundles remain the current mechanism.
